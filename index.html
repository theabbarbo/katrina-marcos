About Me

Steady hands behind every smooth closing

I have spent more than 11 years behind the scenes of real estate teams. For 9 of those years, I was the right hand of a single team, handling their new listings, marketing, offers, CMAs, and closing worksheets. Most recently, I worked as a Transaction Coordinator Assistant, guiding files from contract to close in SkySlope, Transaction Desk, and DocuSign.

My career started in the BPO industry as a customer service representative and later as a trainer, which taught me patience, clear communication, and how to stay calm when things get busy.

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology from the Polytechnic University of the Philippines, so learning new systems comes naturally to me.
